# Battery Health

A lightweight, privacy-focused utility to inspect genuine battery health, charge cycle counts, and electrical diagnostics on Samsung Galaxy devices.

---

## How to Use

Battery Health reads diagnostic dumpstate logs generated by Samsung's built-in system service. Follow these steps:

1. **Open the Phone Dialer**
   - Dial `*#9900#` to open Samsung's built-in **SysDump** menu.

2. **Generate the System Log**
   - Tap **Run dumpstate/logcat**.
   - Wait until the prompt says "Dumpstate / Logcat Finished".

3. **Copy Log to Storage**
   - Tap **Copy to sdcard(include CP Ramdump)**.
   - This copies the generated dump files to your device's internal storage in the `log` folder (`/sdcard/log/`).

4. **View Battery Health**
   - Open **Battery Health**.
   - Grant access to the `log` folder when prompted.
   - The app will automatically parse your latest dumpstate log and display your battery capacity, wear level, cycles, and diagnostics.

---

## What It Reads

Battery Health extracts official battery controller registers directly from the Samsung dumpstate log:

- **mSavedBatteryAsoc (Primary Health):**  
  The primary battery health percentage calculated by Samsung's battery gauge algorithm. Represents remaining capacity relative to design capacity (e.g., `96` = 96% original capacity).

- **mSavedBatteryBsoh (Fallback Health):**  
  Secondary hardware battery state-of-health register used if ASOC is unavailable.

- **mSavedBatteryUsage (Cycle Count):**  
  Total charge cycles logged by the battery management system (stored in the log as cycles x 100).

- **LLB CAL / LLB MAN (Manufacturing Date):**  
  The battery pack manufacturing and assembly date recorded in hardware.

- **Live & Peak Electrical Readings:**  
  Instantaneous battery level, temperature, terminal voltage, and recorded historical temperature peaks.
